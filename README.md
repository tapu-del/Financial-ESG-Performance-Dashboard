# Financial & ESG Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing financial performance and ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) metrics across different industries and regions. The goal was to build a clean, professional finance dashboard to track revenue, profit margins, carbon emissions, and market capitalization.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
* **Power BI Desktop** (Data Visualization, DAX)
* **CSV Dataset** (11,000+ rows)

## 📊 Dashboard Features
* **Modern UI:** Soft light gray background with clean white KPI cards and dark blue borders.
* **KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Profit Margin, ESG Overall Score, and Carbon Emissions.
* **Dynamic Slicer:** Filter the entire dashboard by Region.
* **Visualizations:** 
  - Top 10 Companies by Revenue (Bar Chart)
  - ESG Overall by Industry (Donut Chart)
  - Carbon Emissions by Region (Column Chart)
  - Revenue Trend by Year (Line Chart)
  - Market Cap by Industry (Treemap)
  - ESG Breakdown: Environmental, Social, Governance (Pie Chart)

## 💡 Key Insights
* The line chart shows a clear upward trend in revenue over the years.
* The Treemap reveals which industries hold the highest market capitalization.
* Carbon emissions are heavily concentrated in specific regions, highlighting areas for sustainability focus.
